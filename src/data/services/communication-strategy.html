How it runs

The House Method, applied to how you communicate. You finish with a plan your team can actually use.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Interviews with you and your team about the business, your values and what makes you stand out, alongside a review of how you communicate now and who you need to reach.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We turn what we've learned into one clear plan: who you're talking to, what matters most to each audience and the best channels to reach them.

  3. 03

    Create

    The narrative, key messages and writing guide your team will use, so every piece of communication sounds like you.

  4. 04

    Publish

    We walk your team through the plan and the writing guide and answer their questions, so they feel confident using it from day one.

  5. 05

    Learn

    We show your team how to measure what's working and when to review and update the plan, so it keeps evolving with your business.

Questions people ask

How is this different from a marketing plan?

Think of it as the foundation your marketing sits on. Marketing focuses on selling, but it works far better when you already know who you're talking to, what each audience needs to hear and how your story comes across to all of them.

Who in our team needs to be involved?

The people who speak for the business most – usually the founder or director, and whoever runs marketing. And if your team needs it, we train them too: workshops on the messaging, the tone of voice and the channel plan, so they can carry the strategy forward with confidence long after we hand it over.

Will you help us put it into practice?

Yes. We can walk your team through it, or carry it out with you as part of our In-house support.

Do you cover internal communications?

Yes. How you talk to your own team matters as much as how you talk to customers, because your people are often the first to tell your story.
