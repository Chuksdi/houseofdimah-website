Questions people ask

Which platforms do you work on?

The ones your customers actually use. Usually Instagram, LinkedIn and Facebook, and TikTok when it fits. If a platform isn't worth your time, we'll say so.

How many posts will we get?

It depends on your plan. We agree the number together, based on what your audience needs and what we can do well, and it's set out before we start.

Is there a minimum term?

Yes. Social media takes time to build, so we start with a set first period that's long enough to test, learn and see the results. After that, you decide whether to carry on, change the plan or step it up.

Will you reply to comments and messages?

We can. Some clients like us to look after it fully, others prefer to keep replies in-house with our guidance on how to respond, what to say to tricky comments and how to keep it in your voice. We'll agree what suits you.

How will we know it is working?

Every month you get a clear report: what went out, how well it performed and what we will change as a result.
