Questions people ask

How long does a website take?

6 to 8 weeks from the first conversation to launch. Bigger or more complex sites take longer, and we tell you that up front.

Can we update it ourselves?

Yes. We'll agree up front how much control you want, from editing text and images to building new pages from ready-made templates. Then we show you how before launch.

Will it show up in search?

We build every site so search engines and AI tools can read it properly, and write page titles and copy around what people actually search. Nobody can promise rankings, but we remove the reasons a site can't rank.

Do you look after hosting?

Yes. We set up fast, secure hosting and connect your domain. The domain stays in your name, so you always own it.

What happens after launch?

The first week after launch is on us: free support for any questions or tweaks. From there, our Care & updates plan covers fixes, updates and regular checks on how your site is performing. It's priced to suit how much help you need.

Can you redesign our existing website?

Yes. A redesign isn't just a new look. We improve how the site is structured, how easily people find what they need and how clearly it tells your story, while keeping the pages and search rankings you've already earned.
