Questions people ask

Where do you shoot?

Wherever your story is told best. We start by looking at the spaces you have, and work with you to find the ones that show who you are. We work across London, Hampshire and Dorset, and go further afield when the story needs it.

How soon do we get the photos and videos?

It depends on the shoot. We agree a delivery date with you before the day, and if something is urgent, we can get a few favourites to you first.

Can we use the content anywhere?

Yes, on any of your own channels: your website, social media, print and ads. We also give you guidance on how to use it well, so every piece works as hard as it can.

Do you need us there on the day?

It helps to have someone who knows the business on hand, but we plan the day so it doesn't take over yours.

Do you write the captions too?

Yes. Copy is part of it: captions, web copy and anything else the content needs.
