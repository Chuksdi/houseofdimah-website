How a branding project runs

The House Method, applied to your brand. Most projects take 6 to 8 weeks.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Interviews with you and your team, a look at what you have now, and at who you're up against.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Your story and strategy, written down and agreed, so the design has something to say.

  3. 03

    Create

    Logo routes, colour and type. We refine one route with you, and nothing is final without your yes.

  4. 04

    Publish

    Guidelines and files handed over, and the new brand rolled out across your site, social and print.

  5. 05

    Learn

    A check-in to review your brand's rollout and how it's being received.

Questions people ask

How long does a rebrand take?

Most branding projects take 6 to 8 weeks from the first conversation to handing over the files. A refresh can be quicker.

Can we keep parts of our current brand?

Yes. We look at what's already working and worth keeping, whether that's your logo, colours or other recognisable elements, and build on these to strengthen your overall brand identity.

What do you need from us?

Not much. Two workshops of about two hours, so we can get to know your business and the people you serve. Anything you already have, like logos, photos and past materials. And one person who can make the final call, so decisions don't stall.

Do we own the logo and files?

Yes. You get full ownership of the final logo and all the files, so you can use them however and wherever you like.

Can you do our website and social media too?

Yes. Most clients carry the new brand straight into a website and social media, run by the same team.
