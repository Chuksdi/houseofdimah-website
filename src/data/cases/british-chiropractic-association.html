What the data taught us.

People first,
offers second.

Posts built around people and their everyday moments travelled furthest. Our people-led carousels passed 15,000 views, while a straight discount post stalled under 2,000.

Test the trend before
you follow it.

Everyone says “post more video”. When we tested it, photo carousels drove three times the views that Reels did for the BCA specifically. The format that wins is the one your audience proves, not the one the algorithm blogs push.

Have an opinion

Posts that took a clear position earned the most action. Our call for chiropractors to be recognised as Allied Health Professionals drew 61% engagement rate and the most shares of the year on LinkedIn.
